## What a reading like this has meant

- Since 2015, a major corporate backlash episode (national, sustained) followed within two weeks of **34 in 100** weeks that read Elevated, against 21 in 100 after Low weeks.
- Tense weeks make a company story *less* likely to break through and *angrier* when it does. Low news concentration this week means the first effect is weak: stories are landing.
- **Political and institutional at 77.** Political-stance backlash episodes have followed weeks when this reading averaged about 71. Stance-related announcements, political giving and executive commentary carry elevated risk this week.
- **Cost of living at 76.** Cost-of-living anxiety is in its upper range. Price increases, fees and shrinkflation stories will be read against household budgets; lead with value.
- **Geopolitical at 94.** Geopolitical anxiety is in its upper range. Supply, sanctions and overseas-operations stories are being read through a conflict lens.
- **Public safety reading is low.** Marketing missteps have historically become the story in quiet weeks like this one; there is less competing news to bury a misjudged campaign.
